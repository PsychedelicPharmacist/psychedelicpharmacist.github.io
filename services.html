Expert Advice for the Psychedelic Curious

Our services (2022–2025).

Everyone Can Benefit: Grandma, Psychonauts, School Teachers, Medical Professionals, Patients, Counsellors, Guides

Mike Bonertz’s work as a Harm Reduction Advocate included offering consultations, education, support, and advice to the psychedelic curious, expert psychonauts, suffering patients, healthcare providers, family members, psychedelic guides and counselors, and businesses of all types (including retreats, facilitators, and treatment centers) to reduce harm and increase safety.

Grandparents & Family Members

Family members learned about psychedelics to better understand a loved one’s interests, or to look at what the research said about age-related conditions.

Psychonauts

Experienced psychonauts added evidence-based information and harm reduction practices from a pharmacist with sixteen years as a licensed pharmacist and decades of personal experience.

School Teachers & Educators

Educators got factual information about psychedelics, so they could talk with students and follow the evolving research.

Medical Professionals

Healthcare providers learned about the emerging field of psychedelic medicine from a fellow healthcare professional with both clinical and personal experience.

Patients

Patients explored what the evidence said about psychedelic therapies for various conditions, and which options were available or still in trials.

Counsellors & Guides

Counsellors and guides deepened their understanding of psychedelic mechanisms and integration practices, to better support the people they worked with.

Donation-Based Pricing Philosophy

Accessible Services for All

We believed in providing accessible services for those who need them; thus, payment for our consulting and educational services was entirely donation-based. These services were conducted remotely and could be done by phone or video.

Important Note

These consulting services are not intended to replace accurate medical advice or a medical diagnosis from a licensed medical professional. This site is now an educational archive. I am not currently accepting consultations.

R.E.L.’s education and consulting services were not a substitute for medical advice or services, as provided by an appropriately licensed physician, psychiatrist, or similar. We aimed to bridge the gap for folks who may not have immediate access to those services.

Onboarding for Consultations

Our aim with our onboarding process was to ascertain a client’s risk factors for psychedelic therapy (if there are any), and to determine the type of support a client may need outside of what R.E.L. could provide, including references to other professionals if a client’s situation appears beyond the abilities and outside the intended scope of our Harm Reduction services.

Clients who booked a remote Harm Reduction Consultation with Mike received a few items by email to fill out beforehand.

The information asked for in these documents was intended to ensure the safety and well-being of individuals interested in participating in psychedelic experiences. They provided us with a comprehensive understanding of your medical history, mental health status, substance use patterns, goals, and current state of mind.

It was important to be 100% completely honest with all of the answers, as it helped to weigh any potential risks or concerns before proceeding with a psychedelic session.

Consultation Session

During a session with Mike, clients had the opportunity to discuss their own questions, concerns, and goals related to psychedelics. Sessions typically lasted 60-90 minutes and could cover a range of topics:

  • Information about specific psychedelic substances
  • Research evidence for particular conditions
  • Harm reduction practices
  • Integration techniques
  • Legal considerations
  • Medical concerns or interactions

Follow-up Resources

After a consultation, Mike sometimes provided additional resources, reading materials, or referrals based on the client’s needs and interests. Many clients found that a single comprehensive session gave them the information they were after, while others booked a follow-up consultation to talk through their ongoing journey.

Consultation Fees

At R.E.L., we believed Harm Reduction was about meeting the patients where they are, finding out what therapies they are interested in, and what works for them. Access was also a massive priority for us. Not just availability--access. Access to information, especially on alternative healing methods, should not have a price tag.

Our consultation services had no set fee — they were entirely donation-based, with no minimum donation.
