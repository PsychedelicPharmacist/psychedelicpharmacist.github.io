Education and Credentials

The complete record of what I hold, where it came from, and what it does and does not qualify me to do.

Currently in training

Master of Psychotherapy and Spirituality (MPS), Counselling stream — St. Stephen's College, University of Alberta. May 2025 to a tentative graduation in 2028.

A 600-hour supervised counselling practicum (PPSYC 5870) is scheduled for July 2027 to June 2028 in Metro Vancouver. I am working toward registration as a Canadian Certified Counsellor (CCPA) and a Registered Clinical Counsellor (BCACC) after graduation.

Until that degree is complete and registration follows, I am a Student Counsellor in Training. I am not a psychotherapist, a registered clinical counsellor, or a Canadian Certified Counsellor, and nothing on this site should be read as offering therapy.

Education

  • Master of Psychotherapy and Spirituality (Counselling stream) — St. Stephen's College, University of Alberta, 2025–present

What this does not mean
