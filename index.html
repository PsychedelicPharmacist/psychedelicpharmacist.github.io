About Our Services

Psychedelic-assisted therapy is a potentially powerful tool which research suggests may help individuals engage in deep healing, but it doesn’t come without risks.

Depending on your current lifestyle and medical history and the substances used there may be negative interactions that could lead to long-term health consequences. This site aims to promote mindful respect for, and safe use of, these substances.

Research into psychedelic-assisted therapy is genuinely promising for some conditions, and it is still developing.

One of R.E.L.’s methods for harm reduction was advancing the general public’s knowledge about psychedelic therapies, by carefully collecting and disseminating scientific evidence on the safety and efficacy of psychedelic substances. This site preserves that educational work.

Disclaimer: R.E.L’.’s education and consulting services are not a substitute for medical advice or services, as provided by an appropriately licensed physician, psychiatrist, or similar, we aim to bridge the gap for folks who may not have immediate access to those services.
